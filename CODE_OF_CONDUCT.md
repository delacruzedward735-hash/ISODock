# Code of Conduct

IsoDock is intended to be a respectful, practical open-source project.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Critique code and ideas, not people.
- Provide reproducible technical details when reporting bugs.
- Respect contributors' time and differing experience levels.
- Do not publish private information, credentials, or security-sensitive details about other people.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, deliberate disruption, spam, or publication of another person's private information is not acceptable in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, hide, reject, or remove contributions and interactions that violate this policy, and may restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

For sensitive conduct concerns, use the maintainer contact route listed on the CodeDev by Edward website rather than a public issue.
